## 01-piazza-navona.jpeg
Piazza Navona
The square close to where we stayed. The fountain at its centre was designed by Bernini. I thought the four rivers, shown as men, were the Nile, the Ganges, the Thames and the Tiber, but they're actually the Nile, the Ganges, the Danube and the Río de la Plata.

The sculptures were great.
